2. Overview

Night Siege is an offline tower defense game for Android. There is no developer account and there are no in-app purchases. We do not sell data. The game uses the following Google services:

Service Purpose Data
Local save game Store progress Game progress, settings (on your device only)
Firebase Analytics (Google) Anonymous game statistics to improve balance and stability App instance ID, game events (e.g. run start, wave reached, play time), device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country/region
Google AdMob Voluntary rewarded videos Advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interactions
Google Play Games Achievements and leaderboards Play Games player ID, player name, achievements, scores

3. Details

Local save game. Your progress is stored on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Firebase Analytics. We collect pseudonymous usage data to understand where the game is too hard or too easy and where errors occur. The data is analysed in Google BigQuery. We do not link it to your name. Retention: 14 months, then deleted.

Google AdMob. Ads appear only when you voluntarily start a rewarded video (e.g. for a bonus). Google may process your advertising ID and device information for this and, if you consent, show personalised ads. In the EEA/UK the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change it at any time [keep only if the game shows a consent dialog].

Google Play Games. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your achievements and leaderboard entries (e.g. best wave, most kills, best endless time) are stored by Google and shown to other players under your player name, depending on your Play Games profile settings.

Feedback form. The form is run by Google Forms. It is voluntary; please do not enter sensitive information.

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for achievements and leaderboards you actively use.
